# Blade Colours

These badges show the blade colours of the Oxford college boat clubs.
I (Fergus Cooper) drew them for the [History](history.md) page, with AI assistance, as simple rectangles with each blade's tip at the left.
Some designs were redrawn using the [OURCs College Blade Colours](https://ourcs.co.uk/information/blade-colours/) chart as a reference.
Each badge links to its SVG file.

The badges are free for anyone to use, change and share, for any purpose, without asking: I have dedicated them to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
This covers the drawings only, not the colleges' names, arms or trade marks.

I plan to draw new, more accurate vectors of the blades themselves, and would love your help.
If you can, please email [{{ contact.email }}](mailto:{{ contact.email }}) a high-resolution photo of your club's blade, and of any variants, such as 1st and 2nd boats or men's and women's blades.
**Please only send photos after the regatta, which ends on {{ dates.regatta_end | ukdate }}.**
A photo taken square-on against a plain background that contrasts with the blade works best.
Please include anything definitive you know about the design, such as named colours or RAL codes.
I will use the photos only as references for the new drawings, which will also be free for anyone to use under CC0, and won't publish the photos themselves.
