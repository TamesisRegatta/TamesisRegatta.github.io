# Blade Colours

These badges show the blades of the Oxford college boat clubs, each with its OURCs club code, in order of club name.
They were drawn for this site's [History](history.md) page, and anyone is free to use them for anything to do with Oxford college rowing.

They are based on the [OURCs College Blade Colours](https://ourcs.co.uk/information/blade-colours/) chart, redrawn with AI assistance and stylised into rectangular badges, with each blade's tip at the left.
Each badge links to its SVG file.
