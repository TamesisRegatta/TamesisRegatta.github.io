# History

Linacre Boat Club has run the regatta since 2022, when Christ Church Boat Club stopped running Christ Church Regatta.
It began as the Michaelmas Novice Regatta, and became Tamesis Regatta from the 2024 edition.

In each event, the final decides 1st and 2nd place, and the small final 3rd and 4th.
