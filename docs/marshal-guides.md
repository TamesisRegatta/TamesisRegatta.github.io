## (1) Head { #head }

At the Head of the River, upstream of the finish.<br>
**Kit:** bib, radio, clipboard, klaxon, loudhailer.

### Duties

- **Finished crews:** keep them going well clear of the finish, then tell them when and where to spin, so they don't build up by the finish. Send them back down the transit lane.
- **River traffic from upstream:** ask it to wait at Holding Point A until the race in progress is over, and tell the SU. Downstream craft that fit use the transit lane, and must not leave it or cross the river without speaking to a marshal.
- Note any coxing protest, and pass it to Race Desk.
- Answer questions from the public.

### River checks

**Head (1)**, Finish (2), Meadow (3), Boathouses A (4), Boathouses B (5), Tower (6), Univ (7), Greenbanks (8), Longbridges (9), Gut (10), Donnington Bridge (11, intermediate course only).

- **You start it.** Listen for "Let's have a river check, starting from the Head, GO!"
- Nothing to report? "Head, clear." Otherwise say what it is, where it is, and where it is going.
- Report everything on the river, especially swans, kayaks, swimmers and anyone fishing from the bank, but not the event launches, the river inspector, geese, or our crews in the right place.

### Klaxons

During a race, **klaxon at once** if anyone falls in the water, someone in a boat needs First Aid, a dangerous collision is about to happen, something blocks the course, or you hear another klaxon.
Someone lying down in a boat? Tell them to sit up; if they don't, ask why, and klaxon if they need First Aid.

- Sound your klaxon in all directions for at least 10 seconds, holding your radio button down, until everything you can see has stopped.
- Then, on the radio: **your position, whether First Aid is needed, and why you klaxoned.**
- Klaxon fails? Say **"KLAXON! KLAXON! KLAXON!"** into your radio.
- Crews stay stopped until **the SU says racing can resume.**
- **Never say "klaxon" on the radio** otherwise: ask "Is the race still live?", and call a faulty klaxon an "air horn".
- **Never put your klaxon down.**

### Radio

- Hold the button down for one second, to wake the repeater, then speak clearly, and let go.
- "SU, SU, this is Head Marshal, come in." If two people talk at once, everyone hears only interference.
- **Never change the channel, put your radio down, or hand it to anyone.**

### Other information

- River traffic goes to the SU; everything else goes to Race Desk. Battery gone? Use the nearest marshal's radio.
- The Environment Agency's river restriction notice (on your clipboard) lets you direct river traffic into the transit lane. Give the public no other orders.
- Oxford City Council approaches you? Send them to Race Desk.
- Stay until your replacement arrives, and never hand over mid-race. Last shift: wait for the SU to dismiss you, then return your kit to Race Desk.

## (2) Finish { #finish }

On the towpath side, at the finish line, near the Cox Stone.<br>
**Kit:** bib, radio, clipboard, klaxon, loudhailer.

### Duties

- **Decide who has won:** sound the clacker as the winning boat's bow crosses the line, however small the margin. The Results Marshal, beside you, reports the result to Race Desk.
- Dead heat: tell both crews to go straight back to the start to re-row.
- **Appeals:** a cox raises their arm as they cross the line. Note it, and pass it to Race Desk.
- Move crews on up to the Head Marshal, well clear of the line before they spin.
- Hold an N status crew whose bankrider has stopped for an incident, as Race Desk directs.
- Tell the SU about river traffic coming from upstream, and answer questions from the public.

### River checks

Head (1), **Finish (2)**, Meadow (3), Boathouses A (4), Boathouses B (5), Tower (6), Univ (7), Greenbanks (8), Longbridges (9), Gut (10), Donnington Bridge (11, intermediate course only).

## Results Marshal { #results }

With the Finish Marshal (2), at the finish line.
Not one of the numbered safety marshals, and not in the river check: **your job is to get the results right.**<br>
**Kit:** bib, radio (on its own channel to Race Desk), clipboard.

### Duties

- After each race, tell Race Desk **the winner, the station it won on (City or County), and the verdict:** 6 inches, a canvas, 2 lengths, clear water, and so on.
- For example: "Race Desk, result: Linacre won on County, by a canvas."
- **Appeals:** watch for a cox raising their arm as they cross the line, and note it. The Race Committee may ask you what you saw.
- You have no klaxon, but **keep an eye out:** if you see danger, tell the Finish Marshal at once.

### Radio

- Your radio is on **a different channel** from the marshals', and you talk only to Race Desk.
- Hold the button down for one second, to wake the repeater, then speak clearly, and let go.
- If two people talk at once, everyone hears only interference.
- **Never change the channel, put your radio down, or hand it to anyone.**

### Other information

- Stay until your replacement arrives, and never hand over mid-race. Last shift: wait for the SU to dismiss you, then return your kit to Race Desk.

## (4) Boathouses A { #boathouses-a }

On Boathouse Island, at the upstream half, by the **5 landing rafts**.<br>
**Kit:** bib, radio, clipboard, klaxon.

### Duties

- Crews land with **bows pointing downstream**, and may need to walk their boat along the island to reach a free raft.
- Whoever holds a landing boat holds the blade of 6 or 8, **never 2 or 4**, or the stream swings the stern out into the river.
- **Check every boat before it pushes off:** bow ball firmly attached, not just taped; heel restraints, so heels can't rise above the lowest fixed point of the shoe; backstays on the front riggers; hatch covers in place; cox wearing a PFD over all other clothing; after 15:00, white lights at bow and stern, switched on. A boat that fails does not boat until it is fixed.
- **Identity checks**, when Race Desk asks: collect the crew's photo IDs from the cox as they land, and tell Race Desk, who will talk you through the rest.
- **Equipment failure?** Find out what's wrong and how long it will take to fix, then tell the SU.
- Talk to river traffic near you, keep it in the transit lane, and keep the SU informed.
- Look out for boats being carried among the public, and warn both. Answer questions from the public and competitors.
