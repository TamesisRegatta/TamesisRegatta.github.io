# Privacy policy

This policy covers this website, tamesisregatta.org.
It is run by Fergus Cooper, on behalf of the organisers of Tamesis Regatta at Linacre Boat Club.
If you have a question about privacy or analytics, please email us at [{{ contact.email }}](mailto:{{ contact.email }}).

## Counting visits

To see how the site is used, we count visits with [Plausible Analytics](https://plausible.io/){ target="_blank" rel="noopener" }, on a server run by Oxford Research Software Engineering.
The statistics are anonymous and only ever added up: no one is tracked, and no profile is built of anyone.

### What is counted

- which page was visited
- where the visit came from, such as a search engine or a link on another site
- the type of device, browser and operating system
- the country, region and city the visit came from, worked out from its internet (IP) address
- how long the page was open, and how far down it was scrolled
- which PDFs were downloaded, and which links to the draw and to other sites were followed

The briefing slides, under `/briefings/`, are not counted.

### What is not

- No cookies, and nothing else is stored on your device to count visits.
- No personal data: your IP address is used only to tell visits apart within a day, and is never stored.
- No tracking across sites, and no advertising.

### Seeing the statistics

The statistics we see are public, on our [analytics page](analytics.md).
Plausible's [data policy](https://plausible.io/data-policy){ target="_blank" rel="noopener" } explains what it collects and why.

## What is stored on your device

The site keeps whether pages show in light or dark mode on your device, in your browser's local storage, so that each page matches the last.
It is never sent anywhere.

The statistics on the analytics page keep two display settings of their own in your browser, if you open that page: which chart is shown, and its layout.
Nothing else is stored, and nothing that identifies you.

## Other sites

Apart from Plausible's script, our pages load nothing from other sites.
The analytics page is the one exception: it shows the statistics from our Plausible server, in a frame.

Links to other sites, such as the draw on Google Sheets, our GitHub repository, and Linacre Boat Club's website and YouTube channel, take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

## Contact details in our documents

Our documents name the regatta's officials and their roles.
Their phone numbers and personal email addresses are never published on this site: the versions of the documents that include them are sent only to the officials and others who need them.

## Why we count visits

We count visits because we have a legitimate interest in knowing how the site is used, so that we can improve it.
As your IP address is used only briefly and never stored, we hold no personal data about you from your visit.
